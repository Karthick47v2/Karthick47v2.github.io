Alongside my main work I take on a few freelance projects, mostly in healthcare AI. Three kinds of work fit best:

**LLM features for clinical workflows.** Agents and retrieval-based tools, plus the evaluation that shows whether they are safe to rely on.

**Model optimisation and deployment.** Taking a trained model to something fast and served behind an API, using tools like TensorRT, ONNX and Docker.

**Prototype to proof.** Going from raw data to a working model, with an honest read on whether it is worth building further.

**How we start.** A short call to understand the problem, then a scoped proposal. While we work you get regular demos, and at the end the code, documentation and a handover.

I've also built NLP, edtech and game AI systems. Clients so far have been startups, researchers and educators in 11 countries, and once a film director who needed an app built around a screenplay. Most have come back with a second project.
